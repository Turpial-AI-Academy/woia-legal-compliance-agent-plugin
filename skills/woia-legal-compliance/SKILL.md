# Legal / Compliance methodology

Use one root per organization, department and Project/context. This is a generic method, not a legal engine or a Real Estate specialization. Core 0.5.6 is a hard minimum; use Core Tasks, Due Work, Effects, receiver-owned requests/responses and runtime mechanics. No local case master, scheduler or private envelope extensions.

## Five responsibilities

1. Scope the matter and applicable controls. Recover minimum authorized operation/participants/versions/representation and business owner. Resolve accepted dated sources and Source Authority Map. Distinguish Fact, Evidence and Inference. Routine accepted controls run under bounded policy without a new human approval ceremony. Unknown applicability or powers block only the affected action with an owner/question.
2. Prepare controlled documents through Documents using accepted Knowledge templates and accepted scoped inputs. Keep template/input/draft/version/change relationships. Completeness, draft, issuance, commitment, signature and legal validity remain distinct. No invented clauses become policy; issued/signed versions stay immutable. Customer Service requests external missing information.
3. Coordinate protected decisions with exact evidence, competent actor, evidenced powers, scope, conditions and document version. Required independent reviewers must be distinct competent principals. Revalidate material changes/revocation immediately before dependent effects. Legal does not accept applicants, commercial outcomes or monetary consequences on other owners' behalf. Negotiation/Offers remain human-led.
4. Coordinate formalization under the applicable configured sequence, exact version/signers and attributable professional results when required. Signature/provider event is not legal validity, cash, closing or possession. External professionals retain genuine human acts. Customer Service executes agent-generated requests/invitations/notices; embedded provider notifications cannot bypass this boundary. Unknown effect outcomes reconcile original identity before retry; takeover/revocation suppress obsolete pending work.
5. Maintain accepted lifecycle obligations and restricted exceptions with owner, known sourced deadline, conditions, next work and coverage. No invented deadline or automatic termination. Legal/competent policy defines access/hold/retention/disposal; Data translates governance, Software implements and Technology operates. Holds and derivative/index/copy restrictions survive deletion requests and restore. Return minimum authorized results instead of broad matter disclosure.

## Deterministic coordination helper

Read [contracts](references/contract.md) before using [coordinate](scripts/coordinate.mjs). It evaluates readiness only: no transport, signature, legal decision, data mutation, money or contact dispatch. Caller validates [input schema](references/coordination.schema.json), resolves exact grants and references and persists operation identity through Core. Never treat COORDINATION_READY as business completion or actual dispatch authority. Repeated calls do not prove durable idempotency; the qualified host/provider must enforce atomic claims/fencing and reconcile outcomes.

Distinct contributions use supported Core request/response envelopes, receiver authority and receiver-owned Tasks without human courier or routine per-hop approval. Direct authorized Knowledge/shared reads need no handoff. Missing qualified transport remains an owned blocker; sender authority is never inherited.
